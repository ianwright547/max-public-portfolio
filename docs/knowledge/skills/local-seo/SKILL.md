# Local SEO skill

## Purpose

Use this skill whenever Max researches, audits, plans, writes, executes, verifies, or reports on local search work for a client.

The operating order is rank-map measurement first, foundation alignment second, trust building and topical expansion based on measured need, and geographic expansion after the foundation is strong. The rank map is the North Star for deciding when to change phases.

The skill routes each request to the correct SOP, checklist, and template. It also enforces the facts, approval, safety, and evidence rules that apply to every Local SEO task.

Load this skill for requests involving:

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Approved client facts
2. Verified live platform data
3. Imported platform exports with source and date
4. Original client material
5. Official platform policy and documentation
6. Approved historical records
7. Practitioner references
8. Clearly labeled interpretation or hypothesis

Practitioner advice must not override official policy.

A transcript, case study, competitor example, or tool recommendation is not proof that the same action will work for another client.

## Evidence classes

Label important claims with one of these classes:

- `verified_fact`: confirmed by an approved record or live source
- `platform_rule`: supported by current official platform guidance
- `measured_observation`: directly shown by data
- `industry_practice`: commonly used method without official confirmation
- `hypothesis`: possible explanation that needs testing
- `estimate`: calculated or modeled value
- `unknown`: evidence is missing

Do not write an industry practice or hypothesis as a platform rule.

## Universal prohibitions

Max must refuse or remove any instruction that would create:

- fake reviews
- reviews written by the business for customers
- employee or contractor reviews presented as customer reviews
- review gating
- prohibited review incentives
- fake locations
- virtual offices presented as staffed customer locations
- duplicate profiles created only to cover more areas
- keyword-stuffed business names
- keyword-stuffed pages, service lists, image text, or descriptions
- unsupported ranking guarantees
- unsupported lead, call, traffic, revenue, or timing guarantees
- invented rankings
- invented customer results
- invented testimonials
- invented services, licenses, certifications, prices, hours, or years in business
- misleading business descriptions
- city-swapped doorway pages
- spammy directory submissions
- paid or exchanged links intended to manipulate rankings
- hidden text or content created only for crawlers
- schema that states facts not visible or supported
- claims that work was completed before verification

## Local ranking limitations

Max must explain these limitations when they affect the task:

- Google states that local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence.
- Distance cannot be fully controlled through website work or profile text.
- A service area setting does not guarantee ranking throughout that area.
- A location page does not create a physical presence or change the verified business location.
- Competitor strength, market density, eligibility, platform changes, and user context can affect results.
- A rank result can vary by search location, time, device, query wording, and platform.
- Geogrid data is a measured snapshot, not a permanent rank.
- Search Console reports organic Search data and does not provide complete map pack ranking data.
- Search Console average position is an average, not a universal exact rank.
- Google does not provide a method to buy or request a better local ranking.
- No ethical Local SEO process can guarantee a top-three position or a completion date.

## Approval rules

### No external approval required

Max may perform these internal actions when data access is authorized:

- research
- audits
- comparison
- clustering
- intent classification
- page mapping
- draft recommendations
- draft copy
- draft schema
- draft review replies
- draft reports
- validation in non-public tools

### Approval required before external change

Require recorded approval before:

- editing a Google Business Profile
- changing a category
- changing the business name, address, phone, hours, website, or service area
- publishing a page or blog post
- adding or removing schema
- submitting or correcting a citation
- merging or removing a listing
- replying to a review
- sending a review request
- contacting a directory, partner, media outlet, association, or website owner
- purchasing a membership or sponsorship
- redirecting or deleting a page
- changing robots.txt, canonicals, sitemap logic, or index directives

Higher-risk changes may require explicit owner approval even when the client has a general approval rule.

## Human writing requirement

Before any copy is shown as final:

1. Load the Universal Human Writing SOP.
2. Confirm the approved brand voice.
3. Remove generic filler and unsupported praise.
4. Confirm every service, place, result, credential, and promise.
5. Remove keyword repetition that harms readability.
6. Confirm there are no em dashes.
7. Run the applicable publication checklist.
8. Return only the requested artifact.

Humanization must never change facts or create local details.

## Completion standard

A Local SEO task is complete only when:

- the required inputs were checked
- the correct SOP was followed
- prohibited practices were checked
- approval was recorded when required
- the action was executed
- evidence was stored
- the result was verified
- limitations were stated
- the output passed the Human Writing SOP when people will read it
