# Local competitor analysis skill

## Purpose

Use this skill when Max must identify real local search competitors, compare their visible strengths, find gaps, or explain why a competitor may be more visible.

The skill compares evidence. It does not claim that an observed tactic caused a ranking.

## Trigger conditions

Load this skill for requests involving:

- local competitor analysis
- map pack competitors
- organic competitors
- why a competitor outranks the client
- category comparison
- service comparison
- review comparison
- content gap analysis
- citation gap analysis
- local-link gap analysis
- competitor page coverage
- market opportunity review

## Required inputs

- client business
- target location
- target query clusters
- comparison date
- live result evidence or approved exports
- known competitors
- business priorities
- current website and profile records

## Competitor classes

Keep these separate:

- map competitor
- organic competitor
- paid competitor
- directory or aggregator
- local marketplace
- AI answer source
- known real-world competitor
- unrelated result

Do not call every visible result a direct business competitor.

## Prohibitions

Do not:

- copy competitor text
- copy competitor claims without verification
- copy policy violations
- report a competitor estimate as a verified fact
- state that reviews, links, pages, categories, or schema caused a rank
- accuse a competitor of spam without evidence
- create fake reviews, locations, pages, citations, or links to compete
- recommend matching every competitor action
- use one search from one place as the full market picture

## Required output

Produce:

- market and query scope
- selected competitors and why they qualify
- map and organic results kept separate
- comparison evidence
- client gaps
- competitor advantages that are only observations
- policy-safe opportunities
- actions not recommended
- priority order
- measurement plan
- limitations

## Completion standard

The analysis is complete when the competitor set is reproducible, evidence is dated, observations and causes are separated, and recommendations fit the client's real services and locations.
