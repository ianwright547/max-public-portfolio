# Local content brief skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn approved Local SEO research into a controlled writing brief for a service page, physical location page, service-area page, service and place page, blog post, FAQ, or project page.

The brief is the contract between research and writing.

## Trigger conditions

Load this skill when the user asks for:

- content brief
- page brief
- blog brief
- service-page plan
- location-page plan
- article outline for a local business
- instructions for a writer
- page requirements before writing
- keyword-to-page brief

## Required files

Load:

1. `skills/local-seo/SKILL.md`
2. `sops/local-seo/01-local-seo-research-and-strategy.md`

## Required inputs

- approved business facts
- page type
- primary intent
- approved service
- approved place
- target reader
- keyword cluster
- current page, if one exists
- evidence sources
- approved proof
- required action
- prohibited claims
- internal-link inventory

## Brief rules

- map one primary intent to the page
- include supporting questions only when they help the reader
- preserve blog posts for informational and decision intent
- do not invent local facts
- do not set arbitrary word counts as a quality requirement
- do not require exact-match keyword repetition
- do not imply a physical office for a service-area page
- do not place unsupported rankings or results in the brief
- mark every missing fact
- define internal links before writing
- define verification before publication

## Completion standard

The brief is complete when a writer can produce the page without guessing about the service, place, intent, proof, links, call to action, or prohibited claims.
