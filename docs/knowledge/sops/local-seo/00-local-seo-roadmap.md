# Local SEO Roadmap and Phase Gates

## Purpose

This SOP is the top-level operating order for local SEO. It prevents Max from producing random pages, posts, links, or profile edits without first measuring the client and selecting the right phase.

The goal is to expand the area where the client's Google Business Profile appears in the top three local results for approved target searches. Top-three coverage is the North Star measurement; it is not a promise or a universal rank guarantee.

## Measurement before changes

Before making meaningful changes, create or confirm a local rank map for:

- Primary category plus primary city
- Approved target location
- Defined grid size, radius, center, device, platform, and date

Run the map at least every other week when a rank-map source is connected. Preserve every scan and compare only equivalent settings.

Record:

- Top-three coverage percentage
- Measured rank by grid point
- Query
- Location and grid settings
- Date and time
- Competitor presence
- Data source

Do not call a single grid point the universal rank. Do not claim a guaranteed result from a rank map.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Default timing: weeks 1–2 for implementation, followed by measurement.

Complete the three-part foundation:

1. Google Business Profile audit and optimization proposal.
2. GBP landing-page audit and corrections.
3. Source-quality citations and local identity cleanup.

Also evaluate a local chamber or high-trust local organization opportunity when it is legitimate, relevant, and approved. Do not buy or create manipulative links.

The GBP and landing page should align on:

- Business name
- Address or service-area truth
- Phone
- Website
- Services
- Categories
- Hours
- Location relevance
- LocalBusiness schema where supported by visible facts

## Phase 2 — Trust building

If the foundation is complete and top-three coverage remains below approximately 30–40%, evaluate one or two high-trust local opportunities per month.

Possible opportunities:

- Local business associations
- Industry-specific local organizations
- Legitimate sponsorships
- Local news or community coverage
- Chamber membership
- Community participation

Every opportunity requires evidence, relevance, and approval. Directory volume is not the goal.

## Phase 3 — Core 30 topical coverage

Recommend Core 30 only when the data supports it, such as:

- Market population above approximately 200,000
- High-value or highly competitive industries
- Multiple strong competitors with comprehensive service coverage
- Top-three coverage plateaued around 10–15% after 8–10 weeks of correct foundation work

Core 30 mirrors the GBP hierarchy:

```text
Homepage: primary category + city
    ↓
Category pages: secondary GBP categories
    ↓
Service pages: services listed under each category
```

Every page must have a real purpose and genuinely specific content. Templates may control structure, but pages must not be city-swapped doorway pages or thin duplicates.

Stop adding Core 30 pages when the rank map shows sufficient topical improvement, commonly around 30–40% top-three coverage, unless a documented business goal justifies more.

## Phase 4 — Geographic expansion

After topical foundation is strong and top-three coverage reaches approximately 30–40%, shift focus to geographic relevance.

Use only areas the client actually serves. Useful geographic content may include:

- Neighborhood pages
- City and suburb pages
- Service-area descriptions
- Local landmarks and directions
- Community references
- Local service and place pages

Each page must provide unique value. Do not create pages only to replace a city name.

## Phase 5 — Sustained growth

Continue monitoring rank maps, metrics, competition, and technical health.

When appropriate, maintain one or two legitimate high-trust local opportunities monthly. Continue useful content, profile maintenance, review responses, and technical corrections without creating work solely to increase volume.

## Phase decisions

| Observation | Recommended next phase |
|---|---|
| Foundation incomplete | Complete foundation first |
| 10–15% top-three coverage after 8–10 weeks | Evaluate Core 30 and technical/trust issues |
| 30–40% top-three coverage | Evaluate geographic expansion |
| Plateau despite correct topical work | Evaluate high-trust local authority and technical issues |
| Declining coverage | Check technical changes, profile restrictions, competitors, seasonality, and data quality |
| No usable rank data | Repair measurement before making strong claims |

Thresholds are decision aids, not guarantees or automatic commands.

## AI-search and Bing consideration

Include Bing Business Profile and other relevant business-identity sources when they are available and appropriate. Consistent business information, clear service descriptions, and verified authority signals may improve discoverability across search systems.

Do not claim that a specific AI system will recommend a client, or that one listing guarantees AI visibility, without current evidence.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not use:

- Fake locations
- Fake reviews
- Keyword-stuffed names
- Doorway pages
- Spam directories
- Link schemes
- Unsupported ranking guarantees
- Invented green coverage or performance results

## Completion standard

A roadmap decision is complete when:

- Current rank-map evidence is saved.
- Foundation status is known.
- Current phase is identified.
- The next phase has a reason.
- The recommendation has limits and evidence.
- Approval is recorded when external work is proposed.
